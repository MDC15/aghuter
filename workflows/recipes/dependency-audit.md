# Inspect dependency health

**Workflow ID:** `dependency.audit`

1. Identify package manager and lockfile before choosing audit tooling.
2. Inspect declared direct dependencies and project policy.
3. Separate known vulnerabilities, stale versions, and compatibility concerns.
4. Do not install, update, remove, or rewrite lockfiles without approval.

## Completion report

- Outcome and scope
- Commands/tools used and why
- Checks: passed / failed / skipped / inconclusive
- Files changed (if any)
- Approval or follow-up required
